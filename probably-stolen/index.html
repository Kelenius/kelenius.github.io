The calculator tells you the maximum value you can get from these items right now. You may wish to save good berries for later.
"Trader" here refers to the retired winemaker, which has a 15% markup. If you have a seller without markup, add their items to Inventory instead.
"Bad grapes" are overripe (shriveled) and underripe (orange, 2 types).
For water, mark how many 750ml portions you have in total.
The calculator ignores +2 brewing days from not adding yeast.
Empty inputs will be treated as 0. (Except money, which will be treated as unlimited.)

Wine qualities are:
Master Vintage (6): 175 + 5.25/day
Reserve (5): 150 + 4.5/day
Production Wine (3-4): 125 + 3.75/day
Homebrew (2): 100 + 3/day
Bucket Brew (1): 85 + 2.55/day
